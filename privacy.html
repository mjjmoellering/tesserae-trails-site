Tesserae Trails

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tesserae Trails is a single-player puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not handle.

The short version

Tesserae Trails has no user accounts, shows no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites. The game itself plays entirely offline. The app does send anonymous usage analytics so we can understand how the game is played and improve it — details below.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress is saved locally on your device. It includes unfinished boards and undo history, completed levels, best star counts, hint spending, Daily results, streak dates, badges and your preferred campaign. The app does not upload this save or offer cross-device sync. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple or Google settings. Deleting the app removes its local progress. Playing puzzles does not require an internet connection.

Analytics and data collection

Tesserae Trails uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how the game is played so we can improve it. The app sends anonymous usage events — for example when a level or the daily puzzle is completed, when a hint is used, and when an in-app purchase is started, completed, restored, or fails. These events include gameplay statistics (level and section identifiers, star counts, moves used, hints used, restarts, and time spent) and in-app purchase outcomes (the product identifier and whether the purchase succeeded, failed, or was restored). No purchase event includes any card, receipt, or account information. TelemetryDeck also records standard technical details common to analytics tools: app version, device model, operating-system version, screen size, time zone, language and region, and a per-session identifier.

To count installs without identifying you, TelemetryDeck attaches an anonymous identifier derived from your app installation. It is hashed on your device and hashed again on TelemetryDeck's servers with a separate secret, so neither we nor TelemetryDeck can reverse it or connect it to you. Your IP address is not stored. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera; we show no ads; we use no advertising identifier; and we do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell analytics data or use it for advertising.

Your analytics choice

Open the gear on the campaign screen and turn off Share anonymous analytics (iPhone) or Anonymous analytics (Android) to stop future analytics transmissions. The choice stays off on later launches. Turning it back on resumes analytics; events generated while it was off are not collected. Turning analytics off does not delete anonymous events already received by the service. iPhone versions before 2.0 do not offer this in-app control.

Feedback and ratings

If you choose Send feedback in the app, it opens your own email app with a message to us. On Android the subject line is pre-filled with the app version, device model and Android version so we can look into problems; you can edit it before sending. Any rating prompt is shown by Apple or Google, and we never see who rated the game or how.

Children's privacy

The app is rated suitable for all ages. The anonymous analytics described above are not linked to any person and are not used to track anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
