Fally Things Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Fally Things is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android made by Mike Moellering. This page explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, and why.

What the game itself collects

Nothing personal. The game has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email or location, and keeps your progress only on your device. We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not sell any information.

Advertising

The free version of the game offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads.

Google may use this information to show personalized ads where the law and your choices allow. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings. On iPhone, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when asked. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's settings.

Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

You can buy a one-time upgrade that removes ads. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can remove ads and restore the purchase later.

Children

Fally Things is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
